## Additional information on RFdiffusion3 evaluation results

The metadata contains three independent runs, where the first run comes from [Lorenz](https://github.com/lorenzkleiter) and the other two come from [Brian](https://github.com/blt2114).   
Note:
1. The runs were executed under a patched version of RFdiffusion3 with a bug fixed by Lorenz. 
2. The contig specification is a [specified version](https://github.com/lorenzkleiter/RFD3-Motifbench-Fixed/blob/main/scripts/contig_specifications.csv) rather than automatic motif placement.

For more information please refer to [Lorenz' repo](https://github.com/lorenzkleiter/RFD3-Motifbench-Fixed).
